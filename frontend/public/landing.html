How It Works

  1. Create a List — Click "Create New List", name your list, and add participants. Takes under 10 seconds.
  2. Share the Link — Copy the unique URL and send it via text, email, WhatsApp, or any messaging app.
  3. Collaborate in Real Time — Everyone opens the link, picks their name, and starts adding and checking off items. Changes sync instantly.

Features

No email, no password, no account creation. Just enter your display name and start collaborating. Perfect for shared lists where not everyone wants to create an account.
